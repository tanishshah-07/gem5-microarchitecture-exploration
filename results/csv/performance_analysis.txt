METHODOLOGY AND INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------
All configurations use the same 3 GHz clock frequency. Therefore, total CPU cycles are used for performance ranking. Cycle-count ratios are equivalent to execution-time ratios at a fixed clock frequency.

Parameter-wise averages are calculated across the complete factorial experiment matrix. Each parameter value is averaged across the tested values of the other two parameters. These averages show overall trends and do not independently explain parameter interactions.

The best configuration is ranked by total cycles. A very small difference from baseline should not be considered a meaningful performance improvement without repeated-run validation.
