HOW TO INTERPRET INTERACTION GRAPHS
---------------------------------------------
Each line represents one value of the series parameter. The plotted points are mean cycle counts averaged over the third parameter.

Approximately parallel lines suggest that the effect of one parameter is relatively consistent across the tested values of the other parameter.

Lines with noticeably different slopes or crossing patterns suggest an interaction. This means the effect of changing one parameter depends on the value of the other parameter.

These graphs show marginal interaction trends. They do not independently establish the physical cause of observed performance differences.
